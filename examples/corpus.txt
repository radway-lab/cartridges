The fictional Cedar Field Station studies river water in the Willow Valley.
The station opened in 2014. Its director is Mira Chen. A team of six researchers
collects water samples every Tuesday at three sites: North Bridge, Mill Bend,
and Reed Marsh. North Bridge is upstream of the town; Reed Marsh is downstream.
Samples are stored in blue insulated boxes and delivered to the laboratory
within four hours. The laboratory measures temperature, acidity, and nitrate.
The station's solar panels supply power during the day. A battery provides
backup power at night. If the river rises above the red marker, sampling stops
until the water level falls. The public report is released each September.
In 2023, the team replaced plastic sample bottles with reusable glass bottles.
The annual open day is on the first Saturday of June. Visitors must register
in advance. The emergency meeting point is the oak tree beside the main gate.
